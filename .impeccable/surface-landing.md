# Surface brief: landing page (/)

Scope: marketing landing page. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: owner or practice manager of a private dental clinic in India, evaluating in a sales demo or alone on a laptop.
Action: View Live Demo (primary), Book a Demo (secondary).
Proof: the product demonstrating itself (live board, real chat components, funnel). No testimonials, logos, customer counts or result claims exist; all figures labelled demo data or estimate.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A live enquiry board is the hero; it proves "every enquiry answered in seconds" instead of claiming it. Refuses the SaaS hero-with-screenshot and dental teal gradient.
OWN-WORLD: cool paper ground, blue-black board panels, signal yellow for live state and the primary action; Archivo with condensed board lettering; ruled grids, square status keys, split-flap status changes.
STORY: the visitor sees enquiries arriving after hours and flipping to Booked, understands where leads leak, sees how the assistant works and where it stops, then tries the demo.
FIRST VIEWPORT: two-line headline across the top; beneath it, subhead and both CTAs in the left third, the live board in the right two thirds, fully above the fold at 1440×900.
FORM: departure/status board, candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list; seed key bf570802 (degraded roll, no challengers).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
